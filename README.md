# Tech Store Sales Excel Dashboard
An Excel dashboard built around sales data. This dashboard aims to give managers of this tech store insights into how their products are performing across time and cities. 

Data can be found here: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/beekiran/sales-data-analysis?resource=download

The data shows records of purchases made by customers of this tech store across 2019. It includes the products in each order, when the order was made, where it was purchased from, and how much it cost. I added a column to sort the products based on what category it fell under. The dashboard I created provides a summary of what kind of products sold throughout the year as well as which city and time of year performed the best.

Here are the conclusions and recommendations made using the dashboard:
- Sale of accessories sold the highest quantity but yielded lower profits than some other categories, which makes sense as the profit margin of said accessories are lower. Phones contributed the highest number of sales revenue, with both types sold making it to the top 5 products by sales. Appliances neither sold a lot of units nor made a good amount of revenue. Consider discontinuing sales of appliances?
- Sales started rising in October and peaked in December, which coincides with the holiday season. Maybe extra promotion starting in September may be a good idea as people are starting to think about holiday shopping.
